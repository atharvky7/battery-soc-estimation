8. STRIDE INTERPRETATION & METHODOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Official Methodological Statement:
  With STRIDE = 500 samples (~50 s), observation windows generated for the 50-second duration
  are strictly 'non-overlapping windows where applicable'.
  For shorter durations (5s, 10s, 20s, 30s), consecutive windows have positive time gaps between them.
  For the 100-second duration, consecutive windows overlap by 50% (500 samples).
  IMPORTANT STATISTICAL CAVEAT:
  Windows extracted from the same continuous trajectory must NOT be described as statistically
  independent samples. While they do not share sample indices in the 50s case, they originate from
  continuous electrochemical battery dynamics and therefore remain temporally correlated.
